An architecture, structural engineering, sustainability and interiors studio in Gachibowli, Hyderabad — one team, one continuous line from first sketch to last light fixture. Three rooms,
one thread.Arrival, living room, kitchen — the same jali lattice, brass light and marble carried through each, so a house reads as one decision instead of three renovations.

Every ch+partners project starts from the same question: what does this specific family actually do in this specific room, at this specific hour of the day? The answer changes the plan more than any moodboard does — a kitchen island built for one household's breakfast rush looks nothing like one built for a family that eats standing up. Six years in, that question has produced twelve very different homes and one recognisable studio voice.
